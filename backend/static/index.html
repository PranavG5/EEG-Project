    Where the signal lives — ERD/ERS brain map

    Average mu/beta (8–30 Hz) power for each imagined hand and their left−right contrast. Motor imagery suppresses this rhythm over the opposite motor cortex, so the contrast is lateralised around C3/C4 — the physical signature the decoder exploits.
